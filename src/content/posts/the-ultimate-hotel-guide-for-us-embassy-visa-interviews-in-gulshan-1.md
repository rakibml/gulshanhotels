Attending an early morning foreign embassy visa interview in Dhaka can be stressful. Most morning appointments at the **US** [**_Embass_**](https://medium.com/illumination/the-darker-side-of-growing-old-b12973c28d25)***y*, UK VFS Global, or Indian Visa Application Centre (IVAC)** require you to arrive as early as 7:00 AM.

Staying in central Gulshan 2 or Baridhara allows you to skip Dhaka's infamous traffic and walk or take a 3-minute ride directly to the embassy gate.

## Why Location Matters for Visa Candidates

Traffic on Pragati Sarani and Airport Road during morning peak hours can cause unforeseen delays. When booking:

1. **Early Breakfast:** Choose hotels that start their buffet by 6:00 AM (such as The Westin Dhaka or Amari).
2. **Document Printing:** Ensure the hotel has a 24-hour business centre to print last-minute passport copies or interview slips.
3. **Luggage Storage:** Embassies do not allow mobile phones, bags, or electronic devices inside. Staying nearby allows you to leave your belongings safely in your room.

## Recommended Hotels Within 5 Minutes

- **The Westin Dhaka:** 5 minutes drive to the US Embassy.
- **Amari Dhaka:** Ideal for lake walks and diplomatic access.
- **Ascott The Residence:** Situated directly inside the Baridhara Diplomatic Enclave.
