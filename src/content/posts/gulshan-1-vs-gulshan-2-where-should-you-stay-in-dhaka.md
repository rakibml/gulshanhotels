If you are visiting Dhaka for business, an embassy appointment, or a short personal trip, choosing between **Gulshan 1** and **Gulshan 2** is usually the first decision you have to make. Both neighborhoods sit along Gulshan Avenue, are only about 2 to 3 kilometers apart, and are among the safest, most central parts of the city. Yet the feel of staying in each zone—and how much time you spend stuck in Dhaka traffic—can be very different. Here is a practical breakdown to help you pick the right side of Gulshan for your trip.

Quick Verdict: Which One Should You Choose?

- **Stay in Gulshan 1** if your meetings are around Gulshan Avenue South, Hatirjheel, Tejgaon, or Mohakhali, or if you want immediate access to corporate offices, local shopping lanes, and busy commercial streets.
- **Stay in Gulshan 2** if you are visiting embassies or diplomatic missions, prefer quieter tree-lined lanes, want a morning walk near Gulshan Lake Park, or want the widest selection of 5-star international and boutique hotels in one neighborhood.

Staying in Gulshan 1: The Commercial Heart

Gulshan 1 sits at the southern end of Gulshan Avenue. It is noticeably busier during weekday office hours, anchored by **Gulshan Circle 1**, **Police Plaza Concord** overlooking Hatirjheel, banks, telecom headquarters, and bustling shopping streets around the DCC Market area.

Why travelers pick Gulshan 1

- **Closer to central Dhaka hubs:** Getting to Tejgaon industrial and corporate offices, Mohakhali, or across Hatirjheel toward Karwan Bazar and Motijheel is faster from Gulshan 1 than from the northern side of Gulshan 2.
- **Everyday convenience:** The lanes around the DCC Market area and Police Plaza make it easy to pick up groceries, SIM cards, luggage, or casual meals on foot. _(Note: Parts of the old DCC Market complex are under redevelopment, though the surrounding shops and temporary stalls remain active.)_
- **Standout 5-star stay:** For full-service luxury right on Gulshan Avenue, [Renaissance Dhaka Gulshan Hotel](https://gulshanhotels.com/renaissance-dhaka-gulshan-hotel/) is the flagship 5-star property in this zone, offering a rooftop infinity pool, 24-hour fitness center, and multiple dining options.

Staying in Gulshan 2: The Diplomatic & Luxury Hub

Moving north up Gulshan Avenue brings you to **Gulshan Circle 2** and the diplomatic enclave. While main roads like North Avenue and Road 45 still see heavy traffic at peak hours, the inner side streets in Gulshan 2 feel calmer, greener, and more residential.

Why travelers pick Gulshan 2

- **Embassy & consulate access:** Most foreign embassies, high commissions, and visa application centers around Gulshan and neighboring Baridhara are either inside Gulshan 2 or within a 5 to 10-minute drive.
- **Biggest cluster of 5-star hotels:** Nearly all of Dhaka's major international and boutique luxury hotels sit within walking or short driving distance of Gulshan Circle 2:
    - [The Westin Dhaka](https://gulshanhotels.com/the-westin-dhaka/) — Located right on Main Gulshan Avenue (Plot 01, Road 45), well known for corporate conferences, Westin Club rooms, and city/lake views.
    - [Amari Dhaka Bangladesh](https://gulshanhotels.com/amari-dhaka/) — Situated on Road 41, popular with business travelers for its central location, dining, and poolside atmosphere.
    - [Crowne Plaza Dhaka Gulshan by IHG](https://gulshanhotels.com/crowne-plaza-dhaka-gulshan-by-ihg/) — Positioned on North Avenue in the commercial belt of Gulshan 2, offering both standard rooms and longer-stay suites with kitchenettes. _(Tip: Double-check the address so you do not confuse it with Crowne Plaza Residence on Road 35.)_
    - [Six Seasons Hotel](https://gulshanhotels.com/six-seasons-hotel/) — Tucked on Road 96 just a minute's walk from Gulshan Lake, known for its heated rooftop infinity pool and lake-facing suites.
- **Budget-friendly options exist too:** You do not have to pay 5-star rates to stay in this area. Mid-range properties like [Hotel Tropical Daisy](https://gulshanhotels.com/hotel-tropical-daisy/) (on Road 35/A) offer a practical, much lower-cost alternative right inside Gulshan 2.

Airport Access & Traffic Reality

Both zones are roughly **8 to 12 kilometers** from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC), with Gulshan 2 sitting slightly closer to the Banani/Kakoli exit.

- **Early morning or late night:** A car ride from DAC to Gulshan 2 usually takes **20 to 25 minutes** (and 5 to 10 minutes longer to Gulshan 1).
- **Weekday rush hours (9:00 AM–11:30 AM & 5:00 PM–8:30 PM):** Expect **45 to 60+ minutes** depending on congestion around Banani and Gulshan Avenue. Using the **Dhaka Elevated Expressway** (exiting near Banani or Tejgaon/Hatirjheel depending on your destination) can help bypass airport road bottlenecks.

**Local tip:** Even though Gulshan 1 and Gulshan 2 are neighbors, crossing between the two circles during evening rush hour can easily take 20 to 30 minutes. Always book your hotel in the **same zone** where your morning meetings or embassy appointments take place.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

## **Is Gulshan 1 or Gulshan 2 better for first-time visitors to Dhaka?**

For most first-time international visitors, **Gulshan 2** is slightly easier because it has calmer side streets, Gulshan Lake Park, flagship cafes, and the largest selection of international 5-star hotels. However, **Gulshan 1** is better if your business meetings are further south near Hatirjheel or Tejgaon.

## **Which zone is closer to embassies in Dhaka?**

**Gulshan 2** is closer to the diplomatic enclave and most foreign embassies in Gulshan and neighboring Baridhara.

## **Which area is closer to Dhaka Airport (DAC)?**

**Gulshan 2** is slightly closer to Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (around 8 to 10 km away), though both Gulshan 1 and Gulshan 2 connect via the Banani/Airport Road corridor and the Dhaka Elevated Expressway.

## **Can I find budget or 3-star hotels in Gulshan 2?**

Yes. While Gulshan 2 is famous for 5-star properties like The Westin, Amari, Crowne Plaza, and Six Seasons, there are also 3-star and budget stays in the area, such as Hotel Tropical Daisy.

## **How far is Gulshan 1 from Gulshan 2?**

They are connected by Gulshan Avenue and sit roughly **2 to 3 kilometers apart**—about a 5-to-10-minute drive late at night, or 15 to 30 minutes during busy Dhaka traffic hours.
