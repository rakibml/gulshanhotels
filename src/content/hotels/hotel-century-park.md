**Hotel Century Park** (also listed as **Century Park Residence** on major booking platforms) is located at **House 36, Road 117 in Gulshan 1, Dhaka 1212**, tucked into a quieter residential-commercial lane just a short walk from Gulshan 1 Circle and DCC Market. If you want a practical mid-range stay in Gulshan 1 without paying 5-star luxury rates like [Renaissance Dhaka Gulshan Hotel](https://gulshanhotels.com/renaissance-dhaka-gulshan-hotel/), this established property is a solid option in the area.

The hotel offers several room categories, including Deluxe Single, Deluxe Twin, Executive Rooms, Studio Suites, and larger Family Suites. Essential business and travel conveniences are covered, including free Wi-Fi, an on-site restaurant serving breakfast, a fitness center, a meeting room, free private parking, and a 24-hour front desk. Airport transfers can also be arranged on request.

Staying in Gulshan 1 gives you quick access to corporate offices, banks, Hatirjheel, and Police Plaza Concord, as well as a short drive north to Gulshan 2 for diplomatic visits and larger luxury hotels.

**Good to know before booking:** This property goes by both **Hotel Century Park** (on its official signage and website) and **Century Park Residence** (on sites like Booking.com and Agoda), so both names refer to the exact same hotel on Road 117. Valid photo ID or a passport is required at check-in.
