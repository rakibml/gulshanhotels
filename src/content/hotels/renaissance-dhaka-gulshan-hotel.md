Looking for a big, proper hotel in the middle of Gulshan? Then this one is worth a look. It is at **78 Gulshan Avenue, Dhaka 1212**.

It is not small. The hotel opened in **December 2019** and has **211 rooms and suites** across 19 floors, so you are not booking into a tiny place where everyone knows your name. The beds have memory foam, every room has a minibar and a safe, and each one is uniquely designed with local touches like Jamdani-inspired art.

## Rooms & Design

Fancy a swim? There is a **rooftop infinity pool**, a children’s pool, and a full-service spa (**R Spa**), with a rooftop bar (**R Bar**) right there if you want a drink afterwards. There is also a **24-hour fitness centre** on the 19th floor and **five distinct dining and bar outlets**—including **Bahar** for international buffets, **SEAR** on the 18th floor for fusion fine dining, **Sear Bar** (Dhaka's first jazz bar), and the 24/7 **Gulshan Baking Company** deli.

### Pool, Spa & Dining

Small thing, but people love it: the hotel hosts signature **Evenings at Renaissance** cultural and beverage experiences. And parking? Both **self-parking and valet parking are free**. If you have ever circled a Dhaka street looking for a space, you know why that matters.

### **Gulshan 1 Location & Nearby Spots**

Now, the location. **Gulshan Circle 1** is about 5 minutes on foot, around 0.5 km. The Westin and [Amari](https://gulshanhotels.com/amari-dhaka/) are both close by in the Gulshan area. **North End Coffee Roasters** is just a 2-minute walk away, which is dangerous if you love coffee and have a meeting at 9:00 AM. While there are no designer shops inside the hotel, you are right in the heart of Gulshan’s prime shopping and corporate district.

### Meetings & Events

Speaking of meetings, what if you need a space for one? Instead of being attached to a separate convention centre, the hotel houses its own massive event facilities—offering **11 event spaces** (including a **6,567 sq. ft. pillarless Grand Ballroom**) that can host up to **280 people** in the largest hall.

### Practical Check-in & Policy Details

- **Check-in & Check-out:** Check-in is at **3:00 PM** and check-out is at **12:00 PM**.
- **Airport Transfer:** An airport shuttle from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC) is available, though it costs extra.
- **Pet Policy:** Pets are not allowed.
- **ID Requirement:** Bangladeshi guests must present a valid National ID (NID), while foreign guests need a passport and valid visa at check-in.

So, is it a budget stay? No. It is a five-star luxury hotel. But if you want a big, modern hotel with a rooftop pool, a great spa, five dining options, and easy walking access to Gulshan Circle 1, it makes complete sense.
