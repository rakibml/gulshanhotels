Crowne Plaza Dhaka Gulshan is at 6A North Avenue, Gulshan 2, Dhaka 1212. It sits in the North Avenue Commercial Area, close to Gulshan 2 Circle. The area is full of offices, embassies, restaurants and shops, so you will not struggle to find things to do or places to eat nearby.

The hotel has 149 rooms and suites in two parts: the Tower Wing and the Residences Wing. You can pick a standard or premium room, or a twin room if you are travelling with a colleague or friend. The suites are bigger and some come with a small kitchenette. If you plan to stay for more than a few days, those are worth a look.

There are three places to eat inside the hotel: The Flair, The Beast and The Deli. On the rooftop you will find a swimming pool. There is also a gym, free Wi-Fi and parking. If you need a place for a meeting or a small event, the hotel has space for that too.

Other big hotels are close by, like The Westin, Amari Dhaka and Six Seasons. Gulshan Lake Park is nearby if you want a short walk. Shopping places in Gulshan are also easy to reach.

It suits people who come to Dhaka for work, especially if their meetings are in Gulshan. It is also fine for families who want to stay in a well-known area.

Be careful when booking

Be careful when booking! There is another property called **Crowne Plaza Residence**. The name is almost the same, and people do mix them up. Before you pay, check the full name and the address.
