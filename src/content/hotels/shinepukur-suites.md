Shinepukur Suites is a small, 30-room hotel tucked away at Road 143, House 8(C) in Gulshan 1, Dhaka. Since opening in 2020, it has operated more like a quiet boutique guesthouse than a typical big-brand tower.

A stay here moves at an easy pace. The rooms come fitted with Egyptian cotton sheets, down duvets, and a coffee and tea maker, while booking an executive suite gets you soundproofing and a separate sitting area. Early risers have access to a 24-hour gym, followed by a complimentary breakfast buffet.

Location is really the main draw. Hatir Jheel, Gulshan Lake, and Police Plaza Concord are all about a two-minute walk (0.2 km) from the front door, and Gulshan Circle 1 is roughly ten minutes away on foot. Grabbing lunch nearby takes almost no effort—Baguettee Paris, Herfy, and Farmhouse Burger are all within a five- or six-minute walk. If you would rather stay on the property, there is an on-site restaurant, a coffee shop, and a snack bar. Larger malls like Jamuna Future Park and Bashundhara City are reachable too, though depending on Dhaka traffic, that drive can take anywhere from 20 to 45 minutes.

Evenings tend to stay low-key, with a small garden to unwind in and a daily complimentary manager’s reception. Free self-parking and reliable in-room Wi-Fi cover the basics well. For airport runs, Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport is around 12 to 15 km away. Late at night on open roads, you can get there in 25 to 30 minutes—though daytime gridlock will easily stretch that—and the front desk can arrange an airport shuttle for an extra fee.

There are a few trade-offs to keep in mind before booking. Room sizes lean toward the compact side, so checking the exact square footage of your room category beforehand is a smart move. The hotel does not have a swimming pool, cribs and infant beds are unavailable, pets are not allowed, and visitor access is restricted strictly to registered guests. Still, if you want a calm, walkable base right by the lake for a few nights in Gulshan, it does the job well.
