**Staying at THE WAY Dhaka: A Quiet Boutique Escape in the Heart of Gulshan**

Visiting Dhaka for business or a short city break, and worried about the constant traffic noise? Finding a peaceful place to rest right in the middle of Gulshan 2 can feel like a challenge. That is where **THE WAY Dhaka** stands out.

Located at House 10/B/2 on Road 54/B in Gulshan 2 (Dhaka 1212), this upscale boutique hotel sits in a quiet residential lane just off the main Gulshan Avenue. You are only a short walk away from Gulshan 2 Circle, corporate offices, Unimart, and several foreign embassies—yet when you step inside, the city rush stays outside.

Unlike Dhaka’s massive 200-room business hotels, THE WAY Dhaka feels much more personal and relaxed. With just 40 rooms and suites, it never feels overly crowded. You can choose from cozy Deluxe rooms (25–30 m²), slightly larger Executive rooms (29–34 m²), or spacious Premier Suites (56–65 m²) if you want extra space to unwind.

**What makes the stay special?**
For most guests, the rooftop is the real highlight. After a long day of meetings or exploring the city, you can take a dip in the rooftop infinity pool, relax on the open-air Aqua Deck, or recharge at the fitness center with its sauna and steam rooms. In the evening, **Top Of The Way**, the rooftop restaurant, offers a great setting for dinner with a view. If you just want a quick coffee or a casual bite downstairs, the 24-hour **Converse Café** and the open-air **Courtyard Café** have you covered.

**What guests love most**
People who stay here often talk about how warm and attentive the staff are. Clean, modern rooms, reliable Wi-Fi, and a genuinely calm atmosphere make it an easy favorite for repeat visitors. Many travelers also feel it gives great value for money compared to nearby 5-star properties like The Westin Dhaka (just a 5-minute walk away), Amari Dhaka, Crowne Plaza Dhaka Gulshan, or Six Seasons Hotel.

**A few things to keep in mind before you book**
Because it is a boutique property, the spaces are naturally more compact:

- The rooftop pool and gym are smaller than what you would get at a large luxury hotel.
- The breakfast buffet offers a neat, focused selection rather than an endless spread.
- A few lower-floor rooms face nearby buildings closely, so if you love natural sunlight and an open view, be sure to ask for a higher floor when booking.

_(Note: Don't forget to carry a valid photo ID or passport, as it is required at check-in.)_
