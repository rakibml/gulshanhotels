Gulshan 2 is pretty much the pulse of Dhaka’s business and diplomatic life. Surrounded by embassies, corporate headquarters, and some of the city's best cafes and shops, The Westin Dhaka sits right in the middle of the action. Despite being in such a busy neighborhood, the 5-star hotel feels like a quiet escape the moment you step through the doors.

The hotel features 235 rooms and suites spread across its upper floors. If you manage to get one facing Gulshan Lake, the view offers a genuinely refreshing break from the usual concrete skyline. The rooms themselves are spacious and practical, with floor-to-ceiling windows that bring in plenty of natural light. You get a comfortable seating corner, tea and coffee facilities, a minibar, and bathrooms fitted with deep soaking tubs alongside their signature Heavenly bath amenities. If you book on the Westin Club level, you also get access to a private lounge with extra perks and a much quieter atmosphere.

When it comes to dining, having five different spots under one roof makes life a lot easier, especially when Dhaka traffic makes going out feel like a chore. Prego, up on the 23rd floor, is the go-to for authentic Italian and even has a private dining room for intimate gatherings. Seasonal Tastes handles the all-day crowd with an extensive buffet and à la carte menu, working equally well for a quick morning coffee or a long family dinner. For something more laid-back, there is Splash right beside the pool, Daily Treats on the ground floor for fresh pastries and sandwiches, and The Living Room, a relaxed lobby lounge ideal for light snacks and evening drinks. Of course, 24-hour room service is always an option for nights when you just want to stay in.

The wellness facilities are just as thoughtfully put together. The outdoor swimming pool is temperature-controlled, and there is a separate pool for kids, which makes things much easier for families. Right nearby, the Westin Spa offers multiple treatment rooms (including spaces for couples) for massages, facials, and body scrubs, while the WestinWORKOUT Fitness Studio is well-equipped if you want to keep up with your routine.

On the events side, the hotel features ten different venues. The Westin Grand Ballroom handles big weddings and conferences, while smaller meeting rooms with private foyers and a full-service business center cater to corporate groups. Practical details are handled smoothly too. Both valet and self-parking are complimentary, and the staff can arrange an airport shuttle for an additional fee.

Whether you are visiting Dhaka for back-to-back meetings, attending a family celebration, or simply looking for a comfortable weekend staycation, The Westin Dhaka delivers a reliable, easygoing experience right in the heart of the city.
