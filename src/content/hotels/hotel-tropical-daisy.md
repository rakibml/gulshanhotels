If you’ve ever tried to find a decent hotel in Gulshan without spending five-star money, you know it’s not easy. Tropical Daisy is one of the few that makes sense. It’s a small 3-star place on a quiet lane, right by Banani Lake, and it feels more like a guesthouse than a hotel chain. Don’t expect a big lobby or a fancy pool. That’s just not what this place is.

What I like is the location. You’re in the middle of Gulshan, close to the embassies and offices, but the lane itself is calm. No constant horns, which in Dhaka is honestly a luxury.

It works well if you’re here for a short work trip. There’s free Wi-Fi, a proper desk in the room, and you can get to most meetings without spending half your day in traffic. It’s also a good pick if you’re watching your budget but still want to stay in a safe, central area.

It’s not for everyone, though. If you want a huge buffet, a spa or a grand entrance, you’ll find it a bit small. The building is also on the older side, so soundproofing isn’t perfect and you may hear a bit of street noise now and then. And if you’re planning a big conference or a wedding, look elsewhere. The meeting space is fine for small groups only.

So, bottom line: it’s clean, safe and simple, and you get a lot for what you pay. Just go in knowing it’s a small boutique hotel, not a resort.
