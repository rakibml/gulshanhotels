## About Gulshan 2

Gulshan 2 is one of the well-known areas in Dhaka, especially for business travellers and international visitors. The area is home to several offices, banks, restaurants, shopping places and foreign embassies. Gulshan Lake is also nearby, offering a pleasant place to walk and enjoy some fresh air away from the busy roads.

Because of its location, Gulshan 2 is a popular place to stay for people visiting Dhaka for business meetings, embassy appointments or personal trips. It also offers easy access to other important areas of the city, including Banani and Gulshan 1.

## How to Choose a Hotel in Gulshan 2

Before choosing a hotel, think about where you need to go and what matters most during your stay. Here are a few things worth checking:

- **Check the location:** If you are visiting an embassy, office or business centre, check the hotel's exact location on Google Maps. A nearby hotel can save you time on busy days.
- **Consider airport travel:** Gulshan 2 is roughly 8–12 km from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport, depending on your starting point and route. Travel time can change quite a bit with Dhaka traffic.
- **Ask about breakfast:** If you have an early meeting or appointment, check when breakfast is served and whether an early breakfast option is available.
- **Check identification requirements:** Hotels may ask guests to show a valid National ID card or passport. International visitors should confirm whether any additional documents are needed.
- **Compare room prices:** Check the hotel's official website and other booking platforms before making a decision. Room rates can change depending on the date, room type and availability.
