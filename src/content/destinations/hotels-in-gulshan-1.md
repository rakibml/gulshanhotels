Choosing where to stay in Dhaka can completely change your experience of the city. While Gulshan 2 is known for embassies, diplomatic residences, and quiet lake paths, **Gulshan 1 is the bustling commercial engine of northern Dhaka**.

If your visit involves business meetings around corporate headquarters, appointments in the Tejgaon industrial belt or Mohakhali, or quick commutes across central Dhaka, staying in Gulshan 1 will save you hours typically lost in traffic.

The neighborhood revolves around **Gulshan Circle 1**, the central roundabout connecting Gulshan Avenue with the Tejgaon Link Road, Badda, and the scenic waterfront boulevards of Hatirjheel.

Unlike purely residential parts of Dhaka, Gulshan 1 strikes a unique balance between high-rise office towers and leafy, quiet residential avenues:

- **The Corporate Hub:** Multinational corporations, commercial banks, telecom offices, and tech enterprises line lower Gulshan Avenue and the Tejgaon Link Road.
- **Direct Hatirjheel Access:** Just southwest of Circle 1 sits Hatirjheel lake. For morning exercise or evening relaxation, the waterfront features paved walking tracks, lakeside bistros, and water taxis connecting you across central Dhaka.
- **Shopping & Local Food:** Gulshan 1 DCC Market remains one of the city's best spots for imported snacks, dry goods, and casual street food. Right nearby, Police Plaza Concord offers air-conditioned retail shops, electronics, and global dining.
